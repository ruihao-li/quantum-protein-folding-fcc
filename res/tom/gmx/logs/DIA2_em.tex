\section{Methods}
\subsection{Simulation system}
A system of 3381 molecules (10384 atoms) was simulated.

\subsection{Simulation settings}
A total of 0.05 ns were simulated with a time step of 1 fs.
Neighbor searching was performed every 1 steps.
The PME algorithm was used for electrostatic interactions.
with a cut-off of 1 nm.
A reciprocal grid of 40 x 40 x 40 cells was used with 4th order B-spline interpolation.
A single cut-off of 1 nm was used for Van der Waals interactions.
