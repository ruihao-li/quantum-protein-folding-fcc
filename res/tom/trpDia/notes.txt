# and the file .zshrc contains

# so when I open a terminal and go to trpDia, vmd starts where the tcl files are, i.e. "source foo.tcl" then works in VMD.

# The last 6 lines in .vmdrc open an additional tcl console that is better than the first one up in that the up arrow recalls previous commands
# Both have not been good for pasting in more than one line, however, so you want to make small tcl files and source them in.
# The squence in which to call the files is

source load.tcl
source align.tcl
source center.tcl
source spin.tcl
source theta.tcl

# The first one simply sources in dia.tcl, alf.tcl and trp0.tcl, to 
# bring in the diamond lattice, the 1-D helical lattice, and trp-Cage centered in the diamond.
# The second one aligns the first 9 CA of trp-cage to middle the middle of the helical lattice.
# The third one moves the cylinder's axis to the z axis; after this the user needs to click on 
# the big yellow sulfur atom in the center a few times and the hit "r" to rotate about this center
# And spin.tcl does a 360 spin about the z-axis, which is what is happening when we scan theta angles to form RMSD. 

The workhorse is theta.tcl.  This was a challenge to write, so it may take effort grasp.
